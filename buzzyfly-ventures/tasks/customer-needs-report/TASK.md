Every two weeks, follow `customer-discovery`: read new survey answers and
support tickets in every app's `/admin`, send up to 20 one-to-one discovery
emails to small businesses, schools and corporate teams, and post one
question in a relevant community. Send the CEO the top 5 needs with counts,
quotes and what people would pay.
