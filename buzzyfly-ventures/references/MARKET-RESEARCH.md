# Market research: what reaches $100k/month (Nov 2026)

Starting evidence for the Market Researcher. Most figures are founder-reported
or from vendor blogs. Treat them as directional and re-verify before relying on them.

## Small software products at or near $100k/month

| Product | What it does | Reported revenue | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Postiz | Social media scheduling | ~$251k MRR | Stripe-verified on TrustMRR (Oct 2026) |
| Photo AI | AI photo generation | $50k–$132k MRR | Sources conflict |
| PDF.ai | Chat with a PDF | ~$100k MRR | Founder-reported |
| Testimonial.to | Collect customer testimonials | $100k+ MRR | Founder-reported |
| Bannerbear | Auto-generated images | $50k+ MRR | Solo founder |

Common thread: **one narrow job, a useful free tier, and a distribution
channel** (search, social, or an existing audience).

## Base rates (why we build a portfolio)

- About 70% of small software products earn under $1k/month. About 1% earn over $50k/month.
- Median time from first customer to $100k/month is reported at 3–6 years.

## Digital products that keep showing up as bestsellers

Planners (ADHD, homeschool, study), Notion/Canva templates, budget and expense
trackers, educational and therapy printables, resumes, wedding templates.
Typical prices: $10–$60. No first-party sales data is public. Rankings come from
marketplace observation lists.

## Outreach benchmarks

Cold email reply rates in 2026 are reported at a median of ~2% (Belkins), with
3–6% considered good (Apollo) and 5–10% strong (Instantly). US texts and
autodialed calls without consent carry TCPA damages commonly cited at
$500–$1,500 each. Check current FTC/FCC guidance.
