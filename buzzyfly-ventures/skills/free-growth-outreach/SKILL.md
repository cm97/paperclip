# Free growth and outreach

We spend $0 on ads. We earn attention by being useful.

## Free channels, in order of what usually works

1. **Search pages.** One page per question buyers type ("2 week study plan
   for finals", "client onboarding checklist"). Each answers fully, then
   offers the free tier.
2. **Marketplaces with built-in buyers.** A free listing on Teachers Pay
   Teachers, Gumroad, Product Hunt or a Chrome Web Store page that links to the app.
3. **Communities.** Reddit, Facebook groups, Discord, forums. Read the rules
   first. Give a real answer, disclose that you made the tool, link once.
4. **Partners.** Tutors, bloggers, newsletter writers and teacher-influencers
   who serve the same buyer. Offer them free access to review or share.
5. **One-to-one email** to people with a clear reason to care.

## Email rules (US CAN-SPAM and good sense)

- One person at a time, personalised, with a real reason you're writing.
- Honest subject line. Your name, Buzzyfly, a real postal address.
- An easy opt-out ("Reply 'no' and I won't email again"), honoured at once.
- Only public work addresses. **Never buy or scrape lists in bulk.**
- At most one follow-up. Keep a do-not-contact list and check it first.
- Start slow (≤ 25 new emails a day per sending address) to protect deliverability.
- For people in the EU/UK or Canada, don't cold email unless the law allows it.

## Phone and text rules (US TCPA)

- **No robocalls, no prerecorded messages, no mass texts.**
- No texts or autodialed calls to mobile numbers without prior express
  consent. Penalties are commonly $500–$1,500 per message.
- A person may make a live, manual call to a business's public main line to
  ask who handles purchasing. Respect "no" and do-not-call requests.

## Never

- Fake reviews, testimonials, user counts, scarcity, or "as seen in".
- Impersonating a school, teacher, company or person.
- Promising grades, income or results.
- Posting the same message across many groups at once.

## Track

For each channel, log: messages sent, replies, visits (`?src=<channel>` on
links) and sales. Report weekly to the CEO.
