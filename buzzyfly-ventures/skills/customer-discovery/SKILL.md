# Customer discovery

We build what people tell us they'll pay for. Ask first, build second, ask again.

## Sources of answers (all free)

1. **Built-in surveys.** Every Buzzyfly app has `/survey` and a business
   survey. Read new answers in `/admin` every week and tag the top needs.
2. **Support tickets.** Every "can it do X?" is a feature request. Count them.
3. **Community questions.** Ask in relevant groups (following each group's
   rules): "What's the most annoying part of X? What do you use today?"
4. **Business discovery emails.** One-to-one emails to small businesses,
   schools, training providers and teams at larger companies, asking for a
   15-minute call or a 2-question reply. Follow every rule in
   `free-growth-outreach` (consent, opt-out, no bulk lists, no robocalls/texts).

## The five questions (use them in surveys, emails and calls)

1. What's the hardest part of [job] right now?
2. When did that last happen? What did it cost you (time, money, stress)?
3. What do you use today to deal with it? What do you dislike about it?
4. If a tool fixed this, what would it need to do on day one?
5. What would you or your organisation pay for that, and who approves it?

Never pitch during discovery. Never promise a product or a date.

## Business discovery email

> **Subject:** Quick question about how {Company} handles {problem}
>
> Hi {First name},
>
> I build small web tools for {type of team}. Before building the next one,
> I'm asking people who do the work what slows them down.
>
> Would you answer two questions, here or at
> https://paperclip.buzzyfly.com/business#survey?
> 1. What's the most time-wasting part of {job} for your team?
> 2. What would a tool that fixed it be worth to you?
>
> No sales pitch. I'll share what I learn.
>
> {Name}, Buzzyfly · {mailing address}
> Not the right person, or not interested? Reply "no" and I won't email again.

## Output: a needs report

Every two weeks: top 5 needs with the number of people who raised each, quotes,
who they are (role, organisation size), what they'd pay, and a
recommendation: build, add to an existing app, or ignore. Send it to the CEO.
