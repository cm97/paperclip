# Free ad channels

Pay-per-click ads (Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest,
LinkedIn) **charge for every click**. They are never free. Paid campaigns need
the owner's written yes on the platform and a daily budget. Until then, use
these free channels, each with a `?src=<channel>` link so sales are tracked
in `/admin`.

## Always-on listings (set up once, bring traffic for years)

- **Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools:** submit `/sitemap.xml`
  (owner verifies the domain once).
- **Google Business Profile and Bing Places:** for local and service businesses.
- **Directories:** Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, BetaList, education
  and teacher tool lists, local business directories.
- **Marketplaces with buyers built in:** Google Play, Teachers Pay Teachers,
  Gumroad, Etsy (free listing that leads to the app).

## Weekly free "ads"

- **Short videos:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels.
  15–30 seconds: hook, problem, the product solving it, link in bio.
- **Pinterest pins:** how-to images ("2-week study plan") that link to the free tier.
- **Communities:** Reddit, Facebook groups, Discord, Quora answers. Follow each
  group's rules, answer the question first, disclose you made the tool.
- **Free classifieds and local boards:** Craigslist services, Facebook
  Marketplace services, Nextdoor, library and school boards (local services).
- **Partners:** newsletters, bloggers, tutors and creators who serve the same
  buyer. Offer free access in exchange for an honest review or mention.

## When paid ads make sense

Only after a free channel shows the offer converts (sales from visits). Then
draft a small test for the owner: platform, audience, ad copy, landing page,
$10–20/day for 7 days, and a stop rule. Never launch it without their yes.
