# Revenue systems

Pick the system **before** building. The best businesses combine a reason to
buy once with a reason to come back.

## Systems that work for small teams

| System | How it pays | Good for | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-time purchase | Pay once, keep it | Tools people use for a season | $19 Study Plan |
| Upgrade ladder | Free → small → bigger tier | Anything with individual and group buyers | Student → Classroom → Business |
| Subscription / membership | Monthly or yearly | Tools used every day or week, fresh content | Monthly job board, weekly lesson packs |
| Credits / consumables | Buy a pack, use it up, buy again | AI generations, reports, checks | 50 AI flashcard sets for $9 |
| Licences for organisations | One price per team, school or site | B2B, schools | $299 Business licence |
| Done-for-you service | Pay for an outcome delivered by a person or agent | Busy buyers | "We build your exam plan for you" |
| Marketplace / commission | Take a % of each sale between others | Two-sided demand | Tutors booking students |
| Affiliate / partner | Earn a % for recommending others' products | Content sites | Recommended study tools |
| Local service | Per job or per hour | Real-world businesses | Tutoring, cleaning, errands |

**Subscriptions need the owner's yes** (owner rule). Default to one-time plus an
upgrade ladder, and propose subscriptions only for products people truly use
every day or week.

## Earning daily use, honestly

People come back to things that are useful every day. Build that in:

- A "today" screen that gives one clear thing to do.
- Streaks and progress bars that celebrate real progress.
- Reminders the user turns on themselves, at a time they choose.
- New, fresh value each time (new plan, new cards, new jobs, new lesson).

Never use manipulative tricks: no fake scarcity or countdowns, no hidden
cancellation, no gambling-style loot or random rewards, no guilt messages, and
nothing designed to hook children. Trust is what keeps people paying.
