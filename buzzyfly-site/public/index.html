Stop juggling AI chats. Run your agents like a small team.

Paperclip gives every AI agent a role, a goal and a budget. You see who is doing what, what it costs, and you approve the big moves. All from one dashboard on your own computer.

What you get

An org chart for your agents

Hire a CEO, an engineer, a marketer. Claude, Codex, Cursor or any other agent can fill a seat.

Budgets that stop runaway spend

Set a spending limit per agent. Track the cost of every task in one place.

You stay in charge

Agents ask before big decisions. Review plans, approve work and step in any time.

Work that runs on a schedule

Routines keep recurring jobs going while you sleep. Every action is logged.

Install it in one command

  1. Install Node.js 24.11 or newer. Get it free from nodejs.org.
  2. Run this in your terminal. 
    npx paperclipai@latest onboard --yes
  3. Set a goal and hire your first agent. The five-minute guide walks you through it.

No Paperclip account needed. Your data stays on your machine. You pay your AI provider for the agents you run.

It's a good fit if

  • You have several AI agents open and lose track of what each one is doing.
  • You want agents to keep working while you are away, with a record you can check.
  • You want a hard cap on what your agents spend.

It's not

  • A chatbot. It manages agents. You bring the agents.
  • A hosted service. You run it yourself.
  • Magic. Agents still need clear goals and your review.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Paperclip is open source under the MIT licence. The only cost is the AI models your agents use, billed by your provider.

Do I need to code?

You need to paste one command into a terminal. After that you work in a web dashboard, like a task manager.
