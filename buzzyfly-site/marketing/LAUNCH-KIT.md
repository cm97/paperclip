# Buzzyfly Study Plan: launch kit

Everything here is ready to copy and send. Nothing has been sent or posted for you.

## The math to $15,000 a month (honest version)

| Mix | Sales needed per month |
| --- | --- |
| Student only ($19) | ~790 |
| Classroom only ($99) | ~152 |
| 100 classrooms + 270 students | $9,900 + $5,130 = ~$15,030 |

Teachers are the fastest path: one "yes" is worth five students, and teachers
tell other teachers. Typical cold-email reply rates are low (often 1–5%), so
plan on emailing **thousands** of teachers over a few months, not dozens.
There's no guarantee of any revenue. Track what works and double down.

## 1. Teacher email (send one at a time, from your own address)

Send to individual teachers whose school email is public on the school site.
Each email must include your name, a real mailing address, and an opt-out line
(US CAN-SPAM). Never buy email lists. Stop at the first "no thanks".

> **Subject:** A free study plan for your students before the next test
>
> Hi {First name},
>
> I make a small browser app that turns a test date and a topic list into a
> day-by-day study plan, with review sessions spaced 1, 3 and 7 days apart and
> built-in flashcards.
>
> Students can try it free, with no sign-up and no email:
> https://paperclip.buzzyfly.com/app
>
> If it helps your class, a one-time $99 classroom licence gives you one link
> for all your students: https://paperclip.buzzyfly.com/schools
>
> No student data is collected. Plans stay in each student's browser.
>
> {Your name}, Buzzyfly · {mailing address}
> Not interested? Reply "no" and I won't email again.

**Follow-up (5 days later, once only):**

> Hi {First name}, just checking this reached you. The free version is at
> https://paperclip.buzzyfly.com/app if you'd like to show it to a class.
> Reply "no" and I'll stop. {Your name}
